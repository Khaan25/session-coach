It never makes a second set of cards. So B needs no server change.

**The hole:** "push every log except the current session" would also push a chat that's still open in another window. That half-finished chat gets processed, and everything said in it afterwards is ignored for good. Fix: only push logs that haven't changed for at least 30 minutes. The next SessionStart picks up the rest.

## What the app does

You build something with Claude. Along the way you made choices, like "limit uploads per user, not per internet address". Session Coach reads the chat afterwards. It turns each real choice into a **card**: what you decided, why, and a question like "Why per user?"

Later the app asks you that question, and you explain it in your own words. That's how you end up remembering *why* you built things the way you did, not just *that* you built them.

## Grading: who checks your answer

You type your answer and an AI grader reads it. The grader has the right answer (the "answer key"), which you never see before you answer. It gives one of three scores:

- **2, got it**: you explained it.
- **1, partly**: you got some of it but missed a piece.
- **0, wrong**: you missed it.

After grading you see the score, what you missed, the right answer and the quotes from your chat that prove it.

## 1 / 3 / 7: when the card comes back

Think of a ladder with 4 rungs.

| Rung | What it means | If you get it right | If you don't |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Brand new card | Climb to rung 1. Comes back **tomorrow** | Back to rung 1, tomorrow |
| 1 | Seen once | Climb to rung 2. Comes back in **3 days** | Back to rung 1, tomorrow |
| 2 | Getting there | Climb to rung 3. Comes back in **7 days** | Back to rung 1, tomorrow |
| 3 | Almost there | **Owned!** It never comes back | Back to rung 1, tomorrow |

Each time you get it right, the wait gets longer. Your brain remembers best when it almost forgets and then has to dig the answer out. One miss, even a "partly", sends you back to tomorrow.

## Time rules

- "Tomorrow" means your tomorrow, in your own time zone.
- You can't answer a card early. That way nobody can rush the ladder.
- A new card you never answer waits 7 days. Then it stops being offered, so after a break you don't come back to a giant pile.
- If you get everything right, a card is owned about **11 days** after you first answer it (1 + 3 + 7). That's not 7.

## What Kasam wants to change (the product decisions)

1. **Hide the answer until you try.** Today the session page shows the "why" right above the question, so reading it feels like knowing it. New rule: you see only the decision and the question. The why shows up after you answer.
2. **An "I don't know yet" button.** It's an honest "I give up". You see the answer, it counts as score 0, and the card comes back tomorrow. It's there so people don't guess or paste the question into an AI.
3. **A miss drops you one rung, not all the way down.** Today a miss on rung 3 sends you back to rung 1. New rule: a miss drops just one rung (3 → 2).
4. **Upload sessions when you next open Claude Code**, not after every message. Each chat becomes cards once, after it's finished, so you never get duplicate cards.
