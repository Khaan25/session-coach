Session Coach is built and run by Zia Ur Rehman Khan and Kasam Thapa Magar for the 8x Playmakers trial. It is not run by a company. "We" on this page means the two of us.

## Using Session Coach

Session Coach turns your Claude Code sessions into decision cards and asks you to explain them back. You need an account to use it, and you are responsible for what you send from your machine.

## Your content

Your session logs, cards and answers are yours. You let us store and process them only to run Session Coach for you: to make your cards, score your answers and remind you to review. See the [privacy policy](/privacy).

Do not send sessions you are not allowed to share. Secrets are removed before a log is stored, but that removal is not a guarantee: check what your sessions contain.

## Cards and scores

Cards and scores are written by a language model. They can be wrong. A card is a prompt to think about the code your agent wrote, not advice.

## The service

Session Coach is free during the trial, and we don't take payment details. It may change, pause or stop.

If we stop Session Coach, we email you at least 30 days before. On the day it stops, we delete every account and its data. Backups expire within 7 days after that.

We don't charge for anything without asking you first. If a paid plan comes later, you choose whether to join it.

## Ending your account

You can delete your account at any time from your settings page. We may close an account that misuses the service.

## Disagreements

If something goes wrong, email us first and we will try to sort it out with you.
